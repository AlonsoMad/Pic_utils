## Scope

Implemented the three compositing blocks per architecture §5.7 and
implementation-plan §8 (P2-05):

- `src/pic_utils/blocks/builtin/compose.py` (new):
  - `overlay` (`IMAGE`): places the second image at `(x, y)` with
    `over`/`under` z-order, optional white-to-transparent on the **overlay**
    (fixing the legacy defect), optional resize-to-match, and opacity.
    Negative offsets crop at the canvas edge.
  - `double_exposure` (`IMAGE`): `image*(1-f) + second*f`, clipped to
    0..255; `resize_to_match=False` pads/crops the second image instead of
    raising.
  - `conditional_overlay` (`IMAGE`): the supplied mask gates the overlay,
    refined by a luminance threshold and per-channel ranges
    (`strict` = all channels, loose = any channel). A soft mask scales the
    overlay alpha. The second image is padded/cropped to the chain size.
  - Shared numpy compositing helpers (`_pad_or_crop`, `_fit_to`,
    `_overlay_canvas`, `_composite`, `_finish`); no Pillow and no file I/O.
- `tests/blocks/test_compose.py` (new): 19 tests covering placement,
  white-to-transparent on the overlay, over/under, opacity, resize, negative
  offsets, background alpha, double-exposure endpoints/clipping/padding/
  alpha precedence, mask gating (full/empty/partial/soft), threshold and
  color-range restrictions, and size mismatch.

## Deviations

- Workspace policy blocks `git switch`; committed directly on `main`.
- `_pad_or_crop` centers the smaller image rather than anchoring it at the
  top-left; this is the single alignment used by `double_exposure` and
  `conditional_overlay`.
- `overlay` defaults to `white_to_transparent=True` and
  `resize_to_match=True` (the legacy overlay defaulted to no resize; the
  new default favors a full-frame overlay). Both are explicit parameters.
- `conditional_overlay` combines the mask, brightness, and color
  conditions with AND; the mask's soft value multiplies the overlay alpha.
- The output omits the alpha plane when every pixel is opaque, saving the
  extra 48 MB at 48 MP.
